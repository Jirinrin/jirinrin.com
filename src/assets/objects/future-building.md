# The future of JIRI

You take aim, shoot the arrow, and then you let go. So I really don't think too much about my future or my past. But why not put down some things I feel I'm currently moving towards! Because as beautiful as the Eternal Now is with all its Everythingness and stuff, it's ALSO fun to be already partially living in a future in which more and more humans are living in their heart-center, finding ever-deepening love and oneness and also finding their passions and most authentic selves, awakening all kinds of strange gifts while they're at it, the systems of the world are slowly becoming more and more human, friends from other planets are walking around the globe just sharing their Otherness for all to see, and w̸o̴e̷f̵j̸i̶o̷w̴e̸n̵f̷o̴p̸w̶e̴h̷f̵a̸o̷w̶e̸p̴q̷w̵e̸o̴w̶e̷i̸...

The late-summer sun has just risen, and Jiri wakes up in her little wonky elf-hut deep in the ancient Madeiran woods. Lazily she walks out of her house onto the forest floor, saying a vibrant-but-half-sleepy "Hiiii~" to all the plants and animals and fairies around her, filled to the brim with gratitude that she may live another day on this wonderful planet. She floats over to one of the many Treehouses in the vast canopy of the forest; today she's feeling like enjoying her morning meditation in the one that's built high into the Grandmother Laurel Tree, through the center of which runs a tasty flow of energy that always taps her right in to the Will of the Forest -- a lovely, grounded way to start the day. It's deeply important to her to start the day with a moment of intention and connection to the Earth and Spirits that are always gently guiding her through all experiences Life has to offer. And after that moment of slowing down, time to also activate the Body with some tree-climbing action up high in the canopies -- can a purer form of existential bliss exist than to be flowing through the trees, one with the Perfect Flow of Nature?

On the way home, she lovingly waves to some of the groovy soulmates she's found herself living with in this strange little forest commune. She is very loved by the people in the neighbouring towns for the curious elf wizard that she is, and of course for the love that she always radiates, although some still like to keep a bit of distance to her. She has become a mentor figure that many find themselves coming to for all kinds of advice, from worldly worries to the many difficulties the Path of Spiritual Seeking has to offer.

Cheerfully humming to herself, she picks a pear, some raspberries, walnuts, veggies and a handful of herbs from the community garden, thanking the plants for the beautiful harvest this year. Time to take prepare a hearty little breakfast!

The day flies by. Lately Jiri's been immersed in the creation of a new type of magical wand that uses not just one crystal, but a cluster of different glowing crystals carefully made to grow into each other in a specific shape -- it remains to be seen what the effect is, but it's already soooo beautiful, and it seems to vibratie in a way that makes the music in someone's heart directly audible and feel-able! She often finds herself enveloped in a deep flow state, as the many soft smells, sounds and lights of the forests spiral their way around her and work their way through her. It still puzzles her how she even got the request for this wand from a mysterious gentleman figure, who just kind of ran into her on the one day in the quarter that she would set foot in the town of Essaouira, saying she had been the person he was looking for...

"Wowww already this time?", she says to herself as she suddenly notices how the sun is already past the none-point in the afternoon, and she suddenly feels the soft moss floor under her feet again. And exactly as she gets out of the woodwork back into her senses, she sees her partner Mraana walking the forest path from far in the distance. Mraana is a hybrid earthling that's crossed Jiri's path many times, and together they have grown into very close companions, constantly teaching each other many wonderful lessons. Both being a little introverted, they both have their own lifes in their own villages in the forest, but even so their lives, and every once in a while they facilitate a Mushroom Ceremony together.

"How are you feeling about the Ceremony tonight?", Mraana asks, no further small talk necessary as they effortlessly feel each other's inner state. Her slender, slightly gray-skinned face has a wondrous expression that's hard to pinpoint, her white hair seemingly suspended by a ghostly breeze, making her whole look even more of a fantastical painting.

"I feel great. This morning the Mycelium of the Forest was giving off a feeling of calm, mixed with some playful excitement; that usually means things are lined up for a meaningful shift in consciousness! And from the intake, I feel very connected to those two humans who are coming down from Port Lisboa, they seem to really be on a path of Love. I think you and me really are the perfect ones to guide them through their remaining blockages in this experience~!"

In a beautiful shared silence that seems like it could go on forever, Jiri and Mraana prepare a light dinner together. Both of them appreciate just how precious this time is that they are able to share. In the same flow of presence, they move together to prepare the ceremony space -- a cave space dug out under the roots of the Grandmother Tree, with a small spring of iridescent water in its center, and embedded in the walls little pieces of numerous types of crystals, that dimly light up in patterns, depending on how the energy moves through the space. They clean out all the dust, reset the conduits, clear the energy, lay out the musical instruments, make the beds, hang up the ornaments according to their merged intuition for this particular night...
