# The Future of JIRI

The Future. You take aim, shoot the arrow... and then you let go. This metaphor is just to say that I really don't think very much about my future or my past. But still, why not put down some things I feel I'm currently moving toward! Because as beautiful as the Eternal Now is with all its Everythingness and stuff, it's ALSO fun to be already partially living in a future in which many more humans are living in their heart-center, finding ever-deepening love and oneness and also finding their passions and most authentic selves, awakening all kinds of gifts while we're at it... A time when the systems of the world slowly are becoming more and more human, friends from other planets are walking around the globe just sharing their Otherness for all to see... and more such things that are much more fun without spoilers.... 😁

...

The late-summer sun has just risen, and Jiri wakes up in her little wonky elf-hut deep in the ancient Madeiran woods. Lazily she walks out of her house onto the forest floor, saying a vibrant-but-half-sleepy _"Hiiii~ Good morning~"_ to all the plants, animals and fairies around her, as well as to the Sun and the Clouds and the Earth and the Sky, filled to the brim with gratitude that she gets to live another day on this wonderful planet. _"Good morning Flowers~!"_ and the Flowers smile back!

She floats over to one of the many Treehouses in the vast canopy of the forest. Today is a special day, so she's feeling like enjoying her morning meditation in the one that's built high into the Grandmother Laurel Tree. Through the center of this Elder runs a tasty flow of energy that always taps her right in to the Will of the Forest — a lovely, grounding way to start the day. It's deeply important to her to open the day with a moment of intention and connection to the Earth and Spirits that are always gently guiding her through all experiences Life has to offer... 

After that moment of quiet and slowing down — time to also activate the Body with some tree-climbing action up high in the canopies! Jiri moves through the canopy from tree to tree, entering a deep flow state in which her body knows exactly what to do. She pauses sitting on a branch, her bare feet dangling high above the ground, talking to the birds and breathing together with the tree on which she's found herself. She wonders if a purer form of existential bliss than this could possibly exist, One with the Perfect Flow of Nature...

Walking home through the woods while cheerfully humming to herself, she picks some mushrooms and wild raspberries and puts them in her cotton bag, already putting a couple berries in her mouth.

She lovingly waves to some of the groovy soulmates she's found herself living with in this curious little forest commune. Each of her neighbours has been through their own unbelievable life path and has so much to share, and every day she is amazed how all of their paths have somehow crossed in this little village deep in the woods of Madeira.

Jiri is very loved by the people in the neighbouring towns for the strange elf wizard that she is, and of course for the love that she always radiates, although some still like to keep a bit of distance to her. She has become a mentor figure that many find themselves coming to for all kinds of advice, from Worldly Worries to the many difficulties the Path of Spiritual Seeking has to offer.

She picks a pear, a carrot, some walnuts and a handful of herbs from the community garden, thanking the plants for the wonderful harvest this year. Time to prepare a hearty little breakfast! But first, her body sits her down at the stool in front of the magical piano, and she yields to its will, letting the music flow from her deepest Self through her fingers, feeling all the energies and emotions as they pass through... Time blurs and enchanted loving frequencies echo through the Forest, to the delight of all its creatures and inhabitants...

The day flies by. Lately Jiri's been immersed in the creation of a new type of magical wand that uses not just one crystal, but a cluster of different glowing crystals carefully made to grow into each other in a specific shape — it remains to be seen what the effect is, but it's already soooo beautiful, and it seems to vibrate in a particular way that makes the music in someone's heart directly audible and feelable! Doing this kind of work with the wood donated by her beloved Trees, she often finds herself enveloped in a deep flow state, as the many soft smells, sounds and lights of the Forest spiral their way around her and work their way through her. It still puzzles her how she even got the request for this wand: one day in the quarter that she would set foot in the town of Essaouira, a mysterious gentleman figure just kind of ran into her in an alleyway, saying she had been the person he was looking for and that he was willing to pay upfront...

"Wowww already this time?", she says to herself as she notices how the sun is already past the noon-point in the afternoon, and suddenly she feels the soft moss floor under her feet again. And exactly as she gets out of the woodwork back into her senses, she sees her partner Mana walking down the forest path far in the distance. Mana is a hybrid earthling that's crossed Jiri's path many times, and together they have grown into very close companions, constantly teaching each other many wonderful lessons. Both being a little introverted, they both have their own lives in separate villages in the forest, but even so they both feel that their lives are beautifully intertwined, and every once in a while they facilitate a Mushroom Ceremony together.

"How are you feeling about the Ceremony tonight?", Mana asks, no further small talk necessary as they effortlessly feel each other's inner state. Her slender, slightly gray-skinned face has a wondrous expression that's hard to pinpoint, her white hair seemingly suspended by a ghostly breeze, making her whole look even more of a fantastical painting.

"I feel great. I got the Grandmother's blessing, and this morning the Mycelium of the Forest was also giving off a feeling of deep calm, mixed with some playful excitement! That usually means things are in alignment for a meaningful shift in consciousness, right? And at the intake, I already felt very connected to those two humans who are coming down from Port Lisboa — they seem to be on a beautiful path of Love. I feel that you and me really are the perfect ones to guide them through their remaining blockages in this experience~!"

In a delightful shared silence that seems like it could go on forever, Jiri and Mana prepare a light dinner together. Both of them appreciate just how precious this time is that they are able to share. In the same flow of presence, they move together to prepare the ceremony space — a cave space dug out under the roots of the Grandmother Tree, with a small spring of iridescent water in its center, and embedded in the walls little pieces of numerous types of crystals, that dimly light up in patterns, depending on how the energy moves through the space. They deeply clean the space, clear the energy, do a gratitude prayer, lay out the musical instruments, make the beds, hang up the ornaments according to their merged intuition for this particular night...

After all the preparations are done well in time before the guests arrive, the two sit down for a shared meditation in the Grandmother Cave. Their consciousnesses resonate together, attune to each other and eventually merge — an important ritual they always do before a ceremony, so that they are able to give the best shared guidance that they possibly can. ...But then something unexpected happens. There it is again, a shared vision from a different timeline — or is it somewhere in their future? — where they are living together in a quaint little home somewhere in the universe very far from here, and a child (in the vision they intuitively know it's theirs) is joyously dancing through the kitchen, and a not-so-clearly-visible grandmother figure watching over it with bottomless love. They know these kinds of shared visions all too well already, and they always seem to be pointing to the same timeline, although minor variations are in there... But they both know all too well how pointless it is to try to reason about it with how amazingly complex this reality is, so they simply take it as a sign they're on the right path, and thank the Universe for showing them this beautiful image and for keeping to connect them more and more to each other.

Together they sit in the treetops, watching the Sun set. "Byebye Sun, thank you so much for today...", Mana whispers, tears pooling in her eyes again, so deeply thankful for the love-filled vision they were just able to share.

Before they know it, Night has fallen, and Jiri and Mana get a shiver running along their spines at the same time. Seems it's going to be a meaningful night, to all beings involved...
