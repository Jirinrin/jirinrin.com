# _I_ am the _Tree of Life_.  
### Let me tell you a little story about a Tree Elf and her jOURNEY of Eternal Becoming...

In 1997, a little seedling of a Jiri was planted on Earth, in a loving family in the Netherlands. Oh, what a pure presence of wonder and magic! A delicate being, still so tender, so beautiful...

Ever so slowly, she began to sprout and grow roots, growing more and more connected to this world. She began learning these things known as Life Lessons, for better or for worse. She also gained a younger brother and later another one — two new presences who would come to teach her many things!

By the time the Earth had passed around the Sun twelve times, Jiri had grown into a lovely sapling, with many thin but sturdy Branches and Roots of Attachment... She had come to know oh so many wonderful things Life has to offer, and had also grown some deep Insecurities through bullying, heartbreak, and learning that she was not always safe to express her true self. How many experiences one can amass in such a short span of time! 

She was flowing through Life in a state of pure Play and Creation, deeply believing in Magic ("Wanneer ik later groot ben dan word ik Jiri maar dan dat ik kan Toveren!" she told everyone), but at the same time being oh so Afraid of many things. A deeply sensitive being, oh so easily Overwhelmed by such a tumultuous world... 

She would find shelter from this overwhelm in her own World of Imagination, in which many of her own fairytales were being created every day and being poured into things she created. But this fantastical world would not allow her to stay there forever, and the 'Real World' still had many challenges in store for her...

## Downpour

Then came the next major trial in her journey: the initiation of Attending a New School. Having to adjust to that sudden downpour of new routines, habits and obligations, along with not knowing how to make new friends as someone so introverted, turned out to be oh so difficult for her, to the point of being in tears many times those first weeks. Jiri felt more and more disconnected from her surroundings... 

For better or for worse, it was not an option for her to put on a Mask and Act like the Others to fit in. But she needed some way to protect that delicate little fairy-seed inside of her from burning down in such a relentless environment, so she put on another Mask: she started telling herself that she didn't even care, that she wasn't lonely, that she didn't even want to Belong to a group of friends...

And as if that was not enough, there came knocking on the door that turbulent process called Puberty. Slowly, little Jiri had no option but to come to terms with the fact that she was in a Male Body that was growing ever more away from how she felt inside... And so, another layer of 'thick bark' started to grow...

Overwhelmed by so many discomforts at once, all new to her, all ringing their alarm signals, Jiri completely shut down and retreated into her depths, ever deeper into that little Seed that was still somewhere inside of her... A Cocoon of Non-Being. She became less and less able to connect to the physical world around her, as she forgot who she was, feeling less and less in touch with her ever-more masculine tree-body. She stopped talking to her loving parents about anything plaguing her, now fully shut off from love coming from another human being. Life on autopilot. Getting lost in games and matters of the mind. Apart from a few dimly lit moments of beautiful emotion, Jiri was no longer there. On the surface, she looked like she wasn't bothered in the least not having friends and she was performing well in school. But deep inside, a precious flower that desperately wanted to come out was quietly withering...

Jiri turned Twenty Years Old. For the last two years, she had been using the raw Creative Fire of her new Architecture education, as well as her newfound fascination for Japan and the Japanese language, as a very "productive" escape from the Emptiness of Existence, still being in that mentally uprooted state. She had also just joined a student society and had, to her great delight, met some people whom she felt somewhat connected to. What a Dawn-Light of Hope on the horizon were these passions and friends to her... She held on. But still unbeknownst to Jiri, something had been growing out of sight, at the very tip of her roots... Jiri was starting to realise she might be a _'she'_...!

And then, Suddenly, something started to shift very rapidly. A whole barrage of catalytical events and encounters worked its way through her. And then, after an enriching, experimental semester rooted in British university grounds, it was all clear to her, even if she was still constantly plagued by haggering self-doubt. Within one month of coming back home, Jiri's entire world had turned upside down. Not just because she had just found a more true identity that she was slowly growing into, but why not, also because she was becoming totally disillusioned with the world of Architecture!

## Eclipse

The world turned dark. Not because it was, but because the light had to be blocked off for a while so Jiri could properly turn inside. As her sense perception went deep into her root system and she became completely unavailable to the outside world, gradually she came to terms with the fact that she'd been living a lie for many years. She'd grown into an outwardly tall-standing tree, that on the inside was completely hollow. It was time to strip away everything she had been holding onto: all those old stories and visions of a future that would never be, all those layers of identity... And so, as the Hollow Tree was burning down, a very important moment was slowly approaching. She would soon take her first glimpse since she'd been born into this world... Of her Core. 

After months of Soul-Steering through this difficult but important Dark Night, little by little, Jiri started to See again, with a newfound Self-Knowing and Clarity. The time was coming to find back contact with her Self after all those years, and to make a new plan. It was not going to be easy, but there had never been another option than to walk this path. She had to strap in for the long haul: it was still going to take many years to change her outward appearance to match how she felt inside, especially with years-long waiting lists for medical support. But she was going to take all the steps she could already take. And this next chapter was announcing itself in a second way: after finishing up her Architecture bachelor in a couple more months, she was going to pivot to pursue a Coding Career!

## Suspension

Jiri's Icey Winter slowly came to an end, although Spring would not follow for a couple more years. Still, her world took on a lot more color. There was so much to be excited about: a whole new universe of interests and self-expression possibilities was opening itself to her!

She impulsively went on her first ever trip to Japan, all by herself — an incredible experience of Becoming and Finding her Power, that taught her many unexpected lessons about herself. And directly after that, she started an intensive Web Development course, which she enjoyed so much that it felt like something entirely different from Going to School!

A new firey passion dragged Jiri along. In the ever-seeming suspended liminality between Winter and Spring, ephemeral Flowers started to blossom on her rejuvenating tree-body. Fuelled by that pure stream of Creative Force moving through her, glassy Apples grew that could not help but fall from her...  
([The original form of this website](https://v1.jirinrin.com) is one of the things that 'fell' from Jiri in that time!)  

Things started to flow effortlessly, in a way that she had not experienced for many years. The first page of the next Chapter was ever-so-slightly turning itself already. Jiri found herself being hired in a traineeship programme at Q42, the company of her dreams! She felt finally able to relax, but was also beyond excited about this little world opening up to her. She eased into this warm bath, a family of Fellow Nerds, which was to become a second home through many phases of Becoming that were to follow. Here, she learned so many aspects of what it means to be a Human, how good it can feel to Grow Up, what it feels like to Belong.  

Slowly, as Jiri started to ask more questions about who she was, she allowed herself to explore. There were still oh so many Explorations of Youth waiting for her now that she was finally coming out of that dream-like uprooted state of Hibernation! And her hands were also itching to explore the limitless horizons of Creating Things with her new Programming Skills!

## Blossom

Her first three years of working at Q42, she lived through many internal revelations, struggles and explorations, while still in a kind of Hibernation, just waiting around for that moment that was to come... And then that moment came! Suddenly it was time for her Tree-Body to start the transition to a more Feminine form. And with that, Spring truly began for Jiri.  

Within weeks of the new feminine Tree Saps running through her veins, she began to feel things she had never felt before, and it felt oh so right... Where the last years, her Self had still been suspended outside that masculine body, she was now feeling more and more Home in her body every single day, even if it was an intense and confronting experience beyond words.

With the pulsating electric force of Second Puberty thrusting through Jiri's body, it was now _really_ time for the Explorations of Youth. She became more and more of a social being. She fell in love. She grew into the passion of Dance. To the frustration but also fascination of her colleagues, she became as free-thinking, unreliable and sporadic as many teenage girls are at the peak of their puberty. She started to DJ and organise dancing events. And so much more... Time began to flow like Water, as life became more and more exciting in this endless spiral of self-nourishing!

Now that all these tectonic changes were completely reshaping Jiri's tree-body along with her entire root system, together with the new breathing room that comes with feeling happy in oneself, something _'new'_ began to grow. A new layer of authenticity emanating out from Jiri's Divine Core. A strange feeling that could not yet have a name... A yearning for something Other. Something New.

Although she would not yet admit it, Jiri was slowly growing out of that comfortable warm bath. But her Soul was already setting everything up behind the scenes...

## Magic

Then finally came the tipping point after all those years of deep self-examination, shadow work, meditation, outward exploration... A book about the possibilities of Psychedelics entered Jiri's life. 

This was the perfect puzzle piece at the time. That child-like fascination with Magic she thought she had dropped somewhere in childhood, directly lit up brightly! "_Wowwww, there was this whole magical Inner World with endless possibilities all along, waiting for me to go explore it!!?_ " Jiri thought to herself, in a rapture of excitement beyond any form of enthusiasm she had known until then. And so, she started carefully planning and researching for her first Inner Journey...

In the years that followed, Jiri's life turned upside-down many times, into a Life of Magical Becoming. Her well-paced inner work with the Mushroom opened door after door, accompanied by an unbelievable sequence of unfolding realisations. Jiri was moving through a Spiritual Awakening! As she was very actively purging and healing old traumas to make the return to Wholeness, something inside her shifted permanently. Dormant intuitions and other abilities began waking up in her body, she began giving her Core full control of her Wheel of Fate, she cared more than ever about her body in a healthy way, and discovered more and more ways to be the Real Jiri in all of her strangeness and playfulness... 

Jiri started to deeply Know that she had never been just that one Tree — she had been the whole Forest, all of which is intergrown into the one giant organism of roots and mycelium, there had never been any boundaries! In fact, she had also been the Birds and the Insects and the Fungi and all the rest, all interwoven in a Web of Life! In fact in fact, she had also been all of the Tree Elves and the Gnomes and the Sylphs and all the rest of them, as well as the Others, interwoven in a higher-density layer of that same Web, or you might even say, a multidimensional Tree of Life, wait, isn't that _me!!? And turns out you can work with everything in this Fabric of Oneness in strange esoteric-astral-telepathic ways!? And wowwww everything is radiating Everythingness and Livingness in such extraordinary ways... And it turns out both Space and Time are an illusion, everything in all locations and all moments is all Here and Now, all touchable from this moment and place!!? And wow, how tangibly one can feel that everything is made out of vibrations...! Ahhh it's so crazy, oh and let's move back to my parents then go to Japan for two months and work remote and have many crazy flowy synchronistic situations and soul-encounters, oh and now that Nine-to-Five Job suddenly feels properly suffocating, seems the urge to follow the Fire of Passion instead of staying in the Cauldron of Comfort can't be suppressed any longer, okay I'm just gonna tell my supervisor tomorrow....._

Okay okay okayyyy, this is all going just a little bit fast, don't you think, Jiri-of-the-past? All in less than a year...

With way too many realisations to properly integrate, and all this excitement about worlds-still-mostly-beyond-her-reach, Jiri had yet to realise that there is a very simple point to all of this: that she has a limited tree-body on this World to experience having a limited tree-body on a World. In all that focus on Everythingness, she had silently uprooted and left her sacred tree-body! 

## Life

...So now, with almost all worldly burdens removed, the stage was set to... Calm down. Ground. 

...Breeeeeathe... 

Wow. What a life. Just how much the world begins to sparkle once you can allow yourself to be fully Here and Now, in full Conscious Presence, not taking anything for granted, being oh so grateful that I _(intentional first-person usage for effect)_ have this Three-Dimensional Tree-Body on this infinitely beautiful dualistic planet, going through this journey of Becoming, having all these worldly sensations, being able to Touch a Plant, Taste an Orange, Smell a Flower, Take a Breath... The immense pleasure of simply walking barefoot through a forest, taking everything in with all one's senses... The pure joy of exchanging a simple loving conversation with one's mother... The catharsis of having a real cry about all that's painful in the world... 

It's all indescribably beautiful. All those same 'normal' things, simply met with more Presence and Wonder, and less Anxiety, less Thinking. 

What a feeling to be discovering how to Surrender to Life, in full intuitive Flow, constantly moving through very difficult and very delightful things alike...... Finding more and more ways to be the very truest version of what _I AM_...

...And that brings us to Jiri in her current form: a Transdimensional Tree Elf! Just trying to live her most authentic life, from moment to moment, without worry about Future Outcomes or Opinions, cruising along in all her weirdness, simply pursuing her Highest Excitement... And at this moment that means expressing herself, sporadically creating all kinds of projects, and putting her heart and soul into facilitating Sacred Mushroom Journeys, helping people love themselves in their most strange real-ness!

And as we speak, a whole new type of flowers are starting to blossom on the new Crystallizing Tree-Body that we still call Jiri. A blossoming of Otherworldly Crystalline Flowers, turning into unthinkably strange fruits, most of which are yet to see the Light of Day......

That was all I have to say today!
