# Pillar of Paradigm

Where the Dark and the Light meet, a Playground of Creation comes into being, infinitely stretching out in all directions, oh so freeing... As these polar opposite forces wrestle with each other for all Eternity, ideas come and go, arising and dissolve, in a Divine Flow. 

The Hard and the Soft, the Masculine and the Feminine, the Ha and the Tha, the Solar and the Lunar, the Inhale and the Exhale, the Thinking and the Creative, the Passive and the Active, the Giving and the Receiving, the Forgetting and the Remembering, the Being and the Not-Being, the Me and the Not-Me...

All of these, ever-more interfolding into an ever-complicating Fractal Croissant... Flowing together in a Cosmic Vortex of Nothingness & Everythingness...

This is the field where the Intellect and the Being limit themselves from their limitlessness, to explore the infinite shapes that can exist within it...

### What does Existence want to explore through Jiri today?

It seems I am, after all, just chilling as another creature of Duality. However, because the experience of being a Jiri is all I can really talk about, I want to talk about how these two extremes seem to be alternating in me!

As I'm becoming more and more of myself bit by bit, I've been finding myself less and less in the muddy waters of inbetween-ness, but somehow also more and more...?

I walk through places/non-places. One moment I am deeply meditative, seeing/smelling/feeling everything in its fullness, breathing, living, flowing exactly as my body wants to flow... And the next moment a surge of energy comes and I take off into a dash and start dancing around and climbing in things and speaking in tongues!  
I talk to people/non-people. One moment I fully feel present with the person in front of me, in their human-ness, fully sensitive to their words and all the stuff beyond the words... And the next moment I'm flying out of my body because the social battery is depleted!  
I dance in spaces/non-spaces. One moment I'm dancing in a state of full abandon, offering myself to the music... Then suddenly my body is exhausted and it's time to meditate sitting still on the ground!  
I vibrate in myself/my-not-self. One moment I am in deep, dark, contemplation, overwhelmed by the everythingness of it all... And the next moment I am without a care in the world, simply enjoying the everythingness of it all without needing to understand it!

The two states of Pure Being without Future or Past: Meditativeness and Play. Being in its Emptiness and Fullness, both in its most natural flow... The Monk and the Tree Elf. Both part of me, both part of all of us!

It seems I love to flick between these two extremes in a squiggly square-wave, without much time spent in-between.

And yet, as the Stuff of Life keeps unfolding, it seems the Liminal is taking up more and more space... Not as Indecisiveness, not as Mediocrity (although both of those occur often enough!), but as the Neither-Or, or you might say Both sides at the same time... A state of holding complexity without it needing to be resolved, without needing it to be a clear black-white picture. Of accepting that sometimes you just don't know anything anymore, that sometimes you have to somehow find peace within the endless todo-lists, insecurities, worries, qualms and pains of Life... Peace with not knowing what's the point of it all... Or you may never be at peace...

And then it's time to go bouncing around again. Treading the Middle Path is the ultimate Buddhistic wisdom, and I'll continue looking for it in all the human aspects of life (health, love, passion, etc), but it seems my path is not to stay perfectly in neutrality but experience a lot of crazy stuff that this world has to offer!

### Topics that have been occupying Jiri's Being

_Self-Love._ Lately I've been feeling so much of it, and it's brought me some realisations... Like that we help ourselves much more by allowing ourselves to 'fall into our weakness', and then using that as a catalyst to _choose_ for the healthier and more conscious option, only because it genuinely feels better in the moment and we are whole beings who care about ourselves; rather than feeling we need 'discipline' or need to be hard on ourselves~!

_Radical vulnerability._ How beautiful to be able to share beautiful vulnerable silence. To be able to show our most fragile selves to each other, as one of the most powerful ways to connect, so much more powerful than getting into arguments and defending ourselves etc...

_Dark vs Light._ All of us have both of them in ourselves, and I does it really serve anyone to try to put people/situations in terms of Good vs Bad, Like vs Don't Like, etc...?

_...And there are probably many more such topics, that just don't pop into my head right now~_
