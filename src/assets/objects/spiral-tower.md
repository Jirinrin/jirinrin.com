# Duality of Jiri

I am a creature of Duality, as we all are of course, but because the experience of Jiri is all I can really talk about, I shall relate to you how more and more, I've been finding in myself an intense oscillation between the two extremes of this existence.

I've been looking for the middle path for a while, but it seems my path is not to stay perfectly in neutrality but experience a lot of stuff that this world has to offer!
