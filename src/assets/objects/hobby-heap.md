# The Exultance of Existence

If there is one saying I do _not_ seem to live by, it's 二兎追う者は一兎も得ず:  
I love jumping about between interests, it's sooo fun to experience so much of what Life has to offer! 

This also keeps me open to many lateral insights, and perhaps a bit more closed off from deep mastery of any one thing... Although Flexibility and Equanimity are certainly valid things to master too~!

Anywayy, it's now time that you read the items you like of this veeery big list of things I feel connected too, and you will no doubt feel some of the passion that's streaming through me～!

### Music

Music is love & life & WHAT WE ARE. Please don't (or do) ask me about all the genres of music that I listen to, because then I'll feel as though I need to properly give everything its own proper place in my expression and I'll get _real_ stressed out trying to come up with a good comprehensive answer. 😁  
What this sentence does is it gives an indication of my passion for the field. Which is quite big. ♪☺️♫  
We're talking music collection of hundreds of gigabytes of organised/tagged music, with scripts that keep it organised, DJ gear always sitting ready for a new mix (even if I haven't touched it in a while), long-ago-bedroom-producer-who-still-has-too-many-synthesizers-laying-around, almost every day improvising for what seems like hours on end on the piano...

Check the [Groove Grove](/groove-grove) for a couple past things I made and collected ♥

### Dance

Part of Music but also part of Not-Music, because it also has to do with the BODY! Whaaaaa, what an amazing revelation it was to discover how much Dance has to offer me. I get to do intensive exercise, somatically work through trauma stored in my body, and most importantly, experiment with ways to express myself in my most "Jiri" way, which can either look beautifully free or strangely unsettling from the outside, but is always oh so very very liberating for me! :D

Dancing by myself is very fun in its own way, especially out in nature, but there is a special kind of magic in dancing at small raves or festivals: putting my all into the dance in such a setting makes me part of the interconnected network that the crowd becomes, synchronized by the music, a unified mass that truly transcends any single human identity... It makes the energy dynamics flowing through a group of humans very tangible in all its indescribable ways; it's probably one of the more tangible ways of experiencing Oneness that I know!

If you're ever at a rave and you see a Fairy that seems to be dancing in a different dimension and doing strange magical rituals at the same time, walk up to them and say hi... It might just be a Jiri!

### Nature & Trees

I wouldn't be a Tree Elf if I didn't find myself in Nature and Trees almost every day of my life! This is super super important to me, as a grounded way to connect to one of the deepest parts of my being... Or more simply put, just because it feels sooooo good! That's more than enough reason hehee~ 😁

_(Ah and do note that we really cannot possibly be 'in Nature', because we ARE Nature!! This is a statement that I've heard many times from different sources, and more and more I'm starting to feel how extremely profound it is 😌)_

No but there is seriously something very special about just spending a couple hours in the Woods. Just walking around, connected to the trees and the animals and the elements... Having a nap, followed by a refreshing little fairy dance... Meditating... Climbing a tree or two, or three, or more! Really taking my time, sitting up there, chillin' with the insects up there, sometimes even taking my backpack up there and enjoying a little Treetop Picnic, enjoying the view... And then Oops, I didn't notice it's already getting dark!

I'm telling ya, it's something spiritual. Out in nature, I feel connected to... something. Something inside myself, something about this world. Just the purest version of me, in my full human-ness, without all that worldly clutter or ego-based stuff, and the trees, just being themselves. The Forest being all one organism, one system. The fairies, you'll never know where exactly they are, but you can feel them...  
That's all I'll say about it for now ☺️

P.S. There are many more parts to connecting with Nature that I could still talk about at length, being: multi-day trekking through Mountains and Forests of this world; the special place in my heart that Mountains have; diving into the Endless Enchanted Worlds of the Ocean... And more~!

### Insect Friends

Once you start to see the Insects as Friends, life becomes sooo fun! Every encounter with one of these fascinating creatures becomes like an encounter with a Pokemon!

Lately I've been particularly drawn to the Dragonflies and the Isopods, they just make me soooo happy anytime I encounter one of them! But really all insects are cuties ✨️🦗💕🦋✨️

Fun ideas for insect-related activities: chilling near some flowers, seeing the Bees buzz around harvesting their sweet nectar; watching the Isopods go about their business in their little village in a rotting branch up in a tree; catching the rare opportunity to see a Spider weave its web in real time; enjoying Stinkbugs trying to climb you like you're a tall-standing tree; staring meditatively at a pond as the many Dragonflies do their magical dance, and waiting until one large one lands on your leg; battling with your old self who would be disgusted, as you see a Cockroach just chilling on the fence of the local park...

### Spirituality

It feels strange to talk about Spirituality as a Hobby or an Interest, but it's no matter, as I put it in this list anyway 😁

So there's this thing called The Spiritual Path, although it's a completely unique journey for everyone, so maybe better to call it A Spiritual Path. Anyway! [This blogpost I wrote](https://kinoko.nosk.be/blog/what-is-the-spiritual-path) explains it in more detail, but in a nutshell, it's the path through the darkness, in which we seek out the most true version of what we are, and with it the most true version of what this world is about.

It's really cool, in this search for Truth, you peel back the layers of what you are, discovering more and more lies about the life you lived! And so we die a thousand deaths. And seriously, some of the difficult looking-inward and 'dark nights' can almost feels like a physical death. But we also rebirth a thousand times! And so, a new You gradually reveals itself. More and more, you become a being whose identity doesn't depend on someone else's validation, who notices when an old fear-based belief triggers and finds a way to transmute it into something love-based, who takes responsibility for their life, takes care of your body and mind, who others experience as oh so genuine and loving... More and more, you start to embody Your True Self!

And as someone who has been walking this path for some time now, I'll tell you: It gets real strange at some point! Just about when a couple layers of Spiritual Insights have entered your system, things take a turn... Time starts to flow "in the Now" in ways that make a Year feel like a Flash and a Flash feel like a Year... Feeling into Cosmic Truths becomes second nature... Life reveals itself as a constant chain of experiences that all have meaning and a sparkling beauty and something important to learn to them, even (especially!) the difficult ones... So many mundane things turn into some of the most refreshing things Life has to offer... Magic and playfulness start to show themselves in the everyday so much that you forget what it used to feel like to be a disenchanted adult... So many strange 'coincidences' start to happen that you stop being able to rationalise it... A deeeep gratitude starts to bloom, for just having this crazy turbulent life on this beautiful planet, with so many beautiful things that we're only able to experience in these 3D bodies... Small explosions of fractalized self-love start to occur more and more regularly... (Your experience may vary slightly 😊)

Blablabla rambling about spirituality is fun! This is just to illustrate that Spirituality is not so much a 'part of life' to me anymore -- rather it is Life Itself! It's an immensely intense experience, interspersed by plenty of incredibly difficult periods, but WOW is it beautiful! I'd not have it any other way!! :D

Spiritual activities starter kit (30% joking/stereotyping, 70% not at all joking :) Two years ago I couldn't have imagined I'd be typing this list xD):  
Meditating; reading books by spiritual teachers; walking barefoot in total silence through nature; learning about esoteric traditions like the Tarot/Kabbalah; finding opportunities to let your Inner Child feel loved; embodying Love; learning to talk to the Animals and the Trees and the Elementals; learning Astral Travel into other Places and Dimensions; building a two-sided relationship with your Spirit Guides; vibing with interdimensional intelligences; getting better at Telepathy; seeing who will stick with you when you regularly speak in Tongues (maybe this is just a Jiri thing 😁)...  
Well I certainly haven't been able to fully do all of these yet, but just see it as a reference for how much your horizons can expand once you see what things are possible in the world!

And if you're more material-minded and this all feels a little bit much, it might be a fun bridge for you to go watch the Telepathy Tapes docu/podcast~

### The Mushroom

Who or What is The Mushroom? You shall never quite know, unless you embark on a Journey with it... And so I did. On multiple different occasions over the past years, once every couple months, I have been working with the Mushroom, with Intention & Reverence & Playfulness. When approached in such a sacred way, this _Plant Teacher_ opens gates into the Other, toward Soul Expansion but also diving into deeeep Strangeness...  

And boy, it turned my world upside down, showed me a completely new way of looking at the world and set into motion a whole journey of spiritual seeking, healing and soul-searching! To quote what I wrote on the website for [Project KINOKO](https://kinoko.nosk.be):

_"The Mushroom aided me deeply on the spiritual path; brought me in touch with my most authentic dorky self, worrying less about others' opinions; gave me the self-love to start the work of healing my childhood trauma; led me to seek out a much healthier lifestyle; made me see the world more playfully and optimistically; made me live more in the Now; tapped me into my inner intuition, which led me to quit my previous job to go do something personally important to me in the world..._

_Woww that's a whole pandemonium of changes in a life that was already plenty turbulent! My current life is certainly not a calm oasis by any means, but the days in which I couldn't imagine a happier self are becoming more and more frequent! 😊 *(Having said that, it's certainly not been an easy journey to get here, so don't go thinking the Mushroom will fix you instantly! Doing the difficult shadow work over the years was most essential to get to this place.)*"_

The Psychedelic Path is not the path for everyone, but to those who feel called to it, it has innumerable gifts to offer. ✨️🍄✨️
