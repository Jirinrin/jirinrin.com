During COVID, I was looking for a way to play the arcade game Dancerush Stardom at home. Why, you ask? Because it's soooo cool! :D It teaches you all these crazy cool dance moves, it's one of the funnest ways I know to have very intensive exercise, and it has all these funky songs to groove out to!

Then there was this channel on YouTube which has screen captures of the game playing through all its songs, and it turned out so fun to just dance along with the videos, even without having the arcacde cabinet or the interactive dancefloor or anything!

...Sooooo, one thing leading to another, as it does, over the course of weeks of passionate programming and finetuning over the next half-year, that turned into this website which lists all those videos in a UI that emulates Dancerush Stardom's as accurately as possible! Have a look, it's genuinely sooo fun <3

[Visit Project DANSURANDO](https://dansurando.jirinrin.com)
