# IRUKA DRIVE

A rhythm game to make it genuinely fun to practice typing. I made this back in the day when I deeply wanted to become a game developer, putting a lot of ambition for scale into it, allowing people to add their own songs and make their own charts similar to a rhythm game like osu! or SDVX.

...That said, it never made it past the alpha-test-v1-version, but I still very much believe in the concept! ☺️

Try it out: https://irukadrive.jirinrin.com/
