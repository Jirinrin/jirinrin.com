# KARAOKEQ

A fun custom system to find/queue songs on your local modded Ultrastar Deluxe karaoke laptop! I've crafted it with love and passion to my own wants and needs and it has been battle-tested in my previous student society and my previous work place Q42, going through many iterations. I've semi-recently built it out to also support more separate groups using it with their own song lists etc, but that ended up never really happening 😁

Visit here: karaoke.jirinrin.com
